# Why your laptop fan gets loud

Great question! There are a number of reasons why a laptop fan might start to run loudly, and the most common one is that the processor is working hard.

- The processor gets hot when it runs many programs at once, and the fan spins faster to move the heat out of the case, which is why it gets loud.
- Dust blocks the vents over time, so the fan has to work harder to move the same amount of air through the machine.
- Soft surfaces such as a bed or a sofa cover the vents and trap heat inside.
