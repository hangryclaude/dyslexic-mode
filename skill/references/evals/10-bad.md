## How to reset your router

Resetting a router is quite simple, but there are a few things that you should be aware of before you start, because a reset will erase your Wi-Fi name and password.

1. Find the small reset button on the back of the router, which is usually a tiny hole that you need a paperclip to press.
2. Hold the button down for about 10 seconds until the lights on the front start to blink, and then let go of it.
3. Wait 2 minutes while the router restarts.
