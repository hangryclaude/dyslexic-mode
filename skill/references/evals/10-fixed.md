Press and hold the reset button for 10 seconds.

A reset erases your Wi-Fi name and password. Write them down first.

1. Find the small hole on the back of the router.
2. Press it with a paperclip for 10 seconds.
3. Let go when the front lights blink.
4. Wait 2 minutes while the router restarts.
5. Join the Wi-Fi again with the default name.

The default name is on the label under the router.
