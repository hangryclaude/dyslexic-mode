I've gone ahead and looked into the failing CI pipeline, and after a fairly thorough investigation of the logs, which were quite verbose, it turned out that the failure, which had been intermittently occurring since the dependency bump to vitest 3.2.4 on Tuesday, was ultimately caused by a race condition in the setup fixture in tests/setup/db.ts that was not awaiting the migration promise before the first test was executed. I fixed it by adding an await on line 42, and I also bumped the timeout from 5000ms to 15000ms for the integration suite, since it seemed like the CI runners were slower than local machines. All 214 tests now pass locally (previously 209 passed and 5 failed), and I ran the suite 10 times in a row without a flake. I haven't pushed anything yet, and the change is in commit a3f9c21d8e4b7 on the branch fix/ci-race. Note that I did not verify it on actual CI, so it's possible that there could be remaining issues there.
